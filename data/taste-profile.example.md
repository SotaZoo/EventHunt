# Example taste profile

Prioritize hands-on technical workshops, talks with concrete lessons, and
demonstrations by people who built the systems being discussed.

- Strong fit: a practical workshop or technical presentation with a clear agenda.
- Partial fit: a broad meetup whose technical content is still unclear.
- Weak fit: a sales pitch or generic networking without a specific technical topic.

Consider in-person, online, and hybrid events. Add geographic or scheduling
preferences here if they matter to your catalog.
